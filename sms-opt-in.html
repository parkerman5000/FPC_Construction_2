About our text message program

FPC Construction LLC ("FPC Construction," "we," "us") sends text messages to customers and prospective customers who have requested them. Messages are sent from our business numbers (866) 467-0444 and (803) 884-9305.

What we send

  • Appointment confirmations and scheduling updates for estimates and job visits
  • Estimate and proposal follow-ups you have requested, including a link to view your written proposal
  • Customer-care replies to questions you send us

Message frequency varies with your project (typically 1–4 messages per appointment or estimate). Message and data rates may apply.

How customers opt in

  • By phone: when you call us, our staff or phone assistant asks: “I may send you a text with appointment details. Would that be okay?” We send texts only after you say yes.
  • On our website: by submitting the contact form with your phone number and requesting an estimate or appointment, you agree that we may text you about that request.

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe at any time. Reply HELP for help, call us at (803) 884-9305, or email info@fpcconstructions.com.

Privacy

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Your number is used only to deliver the messages described above through our service providers acting on our behalf.
